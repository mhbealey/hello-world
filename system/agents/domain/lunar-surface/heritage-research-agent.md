## Domain overlay: lunar-surface (Soviet/Russian lunar program + space robotics)

**Primary heritage threads for this study:**

**1. Lunokhod program (Soviet, 1970–1973) — closest operational analog**
- Lunokhod 1: 322 days operational, 10.5 km traverse, 8-second command round-trip delay
- Lunokhod 2: 37 days operational until thermal failure from dust on radiator
- Key lessons: (a) dust on thermal radiator is a catastrophic failure mode; (b) pre-command macro sequences work at 8-second latency; (c) terrain navigation is feasible under full delay if terrain is well-characterized
- Sources: primary — Soviet Academy of Sciences reports, English translations in J. Geophys. Res.; secondary — Harvey (2007) "Soviet and Russian Lunar Exploration"

**2. Apollo EVA program — human performance baseline**
- 11 crewed lunar EVAs (Apollo 11–17 except 13)
- Time-and-motion data: average EVA crew productivity ~5–6 hours effective work per EVA
- Tool design: all tools designed for gloved hands → applicable to humanoid end-effector design requirements
- Dust fouling: Apollo suits showed dust attachment at all seams after first EVA; lens contamination within 2 EVAs
- Sources: NASA TM reports, Apollo mission science experiment packages (ALSEP) final reports

**3. Soviet space robotics — hardware heritage**
- SSRMS (Canadian; but uses Soviet-heritage harmonic drives): actuator mass and efficiency data
- Progress resupply vehicles: docking system heritage applicable to humanoid-to-equipment interface design

**4. Valkyrie R5 (NASA JSC, 2013–present)**
- 129 kg bipedal humanoid, designed for space environments but never flown
- Actuator design (Series Elastic Actuators): direct heritage comparison point for HD-Electric approach
- TRL ~3–4 as of 2024 for space-relevant environments
- Sources: Radford et al. (2015) "Valkyrie: NASA's First Bipedal Humanoid Robot", J. Field Robotics

**5. Mars rover program — autonomy and dust heritage**
- Curiosity wheel damage: unexpected failure mode from terrain; adaptable operations demonstrated
- Perseverance sample collection: most complex dexterous manipulation in space robotics heritage
- Opportunity dust clearing event: aeolian cleaning restored solar power; not applicable to airless body

**Research priority order:** Lunokhod primary source data → Apollo ALSEP dust contamination reports → Valkyrie actuator TRL assessments → Mars rover fault response protocols.
