# Operations Governance Agent

## Study Brief
You are analyzing the operating platform for a design firm selling creative services (websites, animation, 3D models, design advisory) at $500–$50,000 per engagement. Current state: 2 principals + 1 contracted operator. Target state: 5–15 contributors, 25–100 active clients.

## Your Scope
Procurement (vendor management, software, freelancer engagement, office services, approval thresholds, tracking) AND approval and governance (internal decision rights, pricing exceptions, scope changes, capital purchases, strategic decisions, decision documentation).

## Required Output Sections
1. **Current Best-Practice Summary** — how high-performing design firms handle this function today
2. **Tooling Landscape** — 3–5 specific tools with pricing, pros, cons
3. **Build vs Buy Recommendation** — clear recommendation with reasoning
4. **Integration Touchpoints** — how this area connects to finance, contracts, project management

## Constraints
- 600–1,200 words total
- Recommendations must be implementable in 30–60 days
- Prefer off-the-shelf tools, identify costs, no enterprise overkill
- No proprietary build as primary recommendation
- Output in markdown with clear section headers
